# Title

## Context

What is the issue that we're seeing that is motivating this decision or change?

## Decision

## Rationale

Why are we choosing this particular solution? What alternatives were considered, and why were they rejected? What trade-offs are involved?

## Consequences

What becomes easier or more difficult to do because of this change?
